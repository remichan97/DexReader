# Code of Conduct

## The Simple Version

DexReader is a personal project, and we're all here because we love manga and want to build something useful. Let's keep it friendly and respectful.

## Expected Behaviour

- **Be kind** - Treat others how you'd want to be treated
- **Be constructive** - Give helpful feedback, not just criticism
- **Be patient** - We all make mistakes and learn at different paces
- **Be welcoming** - Everyone was new once

## Not Cool

- Being rude, hostile, or disrespectful
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Spam or off-topic content

## If There's a Problem

If someone's behaviour is making the project less welcoming, please open an issue or contact the maintainer. I'll handle it fairly and keep it private.

---

This isn't a massive corporate project - it's just one person who like manga trying to build a reader app. Let's keep the vibe positive.
